# User Taste Profile

## Interest Areas

### Primary Interests
- **AI Agents & Systems**: Multi-agent architectures, LLM applications, autonomous systems
- **AI Products**: Consumer AI tools, productivity applications, AI-powered services
- **AI Research & Tools**: Cutting-edge AI papers, development frameworks, emerging models
- **Crypto & Web3**: Blockchain protocols, DeFi, crypto market trends, decentralization
- **Prediction Markets**: Forecasting mechanisms, outcome resolution, market mechanisms

### Secondary Interests
- **Knowledge Management**: Personal knowledge systems, PKM tools, information organization
- **Indie Business**: Solo entrepreneurship, bootstrapped products, indie maker trends
- **Business Insights**: Market analysis, strategic trends, business models
- **Society & Impact**: AI ethics, societal implications, governance

### Emerging Interest
- **Defense AI**: Military AI applications, defense technology, strategic implications

## Content Preferences

### Format Preferences
- Academic papers and research summaries
- Technical analysis and deep dives
- First-hand experiences and case studies
- Data-driven insights and market analysis

### Quality Criteria
- Original research and perspectives
- Evidence-based claims with citations
- Practical applicability and real-world examples
- Forward-looking analysis and predictions

### Source Priority
1. Primary sources (research papers, official announcements)
2. Technical blogs and expert analysis
3. Industry news and market reports
4. Community discussions and debates

## Content Filtering

### Exclude
- Pure speculation without basis
- Marketing hype without substance
- Duplicate information from major news outlets
- Low-effort social media posts

### Prefer
- Nuanced analysis over hot takes
- Timely but evergreen content
- Contrarian perspectives with solid reasoning
- Emerging trends before mainstream coverage

## Processing Preferences

### Synthesis Goals
- Identify emerging patterns and trends
- Extract actionable insights
- Highlight novel perspectives
- Connect related topics across domains

### Email Frequency
- Daily digest with high-signal content
- Weekly synthesis of major themes
- Alerts for major developments in primary interests
